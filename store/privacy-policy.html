Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Contact: support@rennovaai.com

RennovaAI ("we", "us") provides a mobile app that lets you preview a renovated room with AI, estimate renovation costs and book local renovation vendors. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, or the name and email shared by Google or Apple if you sign in with them; the role you choose (customer or vendor).
  • Profile: name, mobile number, city, optional area, photo, short bio, favourite styles and budget range.
  • Room photos and designs: photos you take or choose, the AI previews generated from them, your design choices (style, colours, materials, room size) and cost estimates.
  • Bookings and messages: booking requests, the agreed price and timeline, stage updates, chat messages with the vendor, and ratings or reviews you leave.
  • Vendor applications: business name, owner name, phone, WhatsApp, services, service areas, working hours, experience, logo and portfolio photos. CNIC is verified in person and is never uploaded in the app.

We do not collect your location (you choose your city), contacts, or payment details.

How we use it

  • To generate AI previews of your room. Your photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI service, which runs on Cloudflare Workers AI (image model by Black Forest Labs). The photo is used only to create the preview and is not kept by the AI service.
  • To calculate cost estimates and show vendors who work in your city.
  • To send your booking request to the vendor you choose and let you both follow the job and chat.
  • To review vendor applications, keep the marketplace safe and provide support.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Payments

RennovaAI does not take, hold or process payments. Customers pay vendors directly for each stage of the work.

Where it is stored

Your account, profile, designs, booking details and messages are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and are protected so that only you (and, for a booking, the vendor you booked) can see them. Room photos and AI previews are stored in a private storage area. Vendor business profiles that have been approved are public inside the app. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Who can see what

  • When you book a vendor, that vendor sees your name, mobile number, city, your note, and the design you booked (photo, AI preview, choices and estimate).
  • Ratings and reviews you leave are shown on the vendor's public profile with your first name only.
  • RennovaAI administrators can see accounts, vendor applications and bookings to verify vendors and resolve problems.
  • Service providers who run the app for us (Supabase for data, Cloudflare for AI, Google and Apple if you use their sign-in, Expo for app updates) process data only to provide their service.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph your room or work, only when you tap to take a photo.
  • Photos: to choose an existing picture.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Profile (customers) or Account (vendors), then tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, photos, saved designs, your bookings as a customer, and your messages, straight away.

Without the app: email support@rennovaai.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my RennovaAI account". We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

When a vendor deletes their account, past bookings stay visible to the customers who made them (without the vendor's details). Server backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Children

RennovaAI is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when this policy changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
