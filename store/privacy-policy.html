RennovaAI ("we", "us") provides a mobile app that lets you preview a renovated room, estimate renovation costs and connect with renovation vendors. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Review this policy with a legal adviser and fill in the bracketed items before publishing.

Information you provide

  • Profile: your name, optional mobile number and city — used to personalise the app and match you with vendors in your city.
  • Room photos: photos you take or choose so we can generate a renovation preview.
  • Design choices and notes: style, colours, materials, room size and any notes you add.
  • Messages and reviews: chat messages with vendors and ratings you leave.
  • Vendor applications: business name, owner name, phone, services, experience and portfolio photos. CNIC is verified in person and is never uploaded in the app.

How we use it

  • To generate AI previews of your room. Photos are sent over an encrypted connection to our AI server, which forwards them to our image-generation provider [PROVIDER NAME] solely to create the preview.
  • To calculate cost estimates and shortlist suitable vendors.
  • To let you book, pay in milestones and communicate with vendors.
  • To provide customer support and resolve disputes.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party ads.

Storage and retention

In the current version, your profile, designs, projects and messages are stored on your device. Photos sent for AI generation are processed and not kept by our server after the preview is returned. [Update this section when cloud accounts and server storage are introduced, including retention periods.]

Sharing

When you book a vendor, we share the details needed to do the job (your name, phone, city/area and design brief) with that vendor. Payment details are handled by our payment partners (e.g. JazzCash, Easypaisa, card processors) under their own policies.

Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph your room (only when you tap "Take photo").
  • Photos — to choose an existing picture of your room or portfolio work.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time and delete all app data from Profile → Delete my data & sign out. To request deletion of any data held on our servers, email support@rennovaai.com.

Children

RennovaAI is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Payments are held in escrow by our payment partners.

Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when this policy changes.
